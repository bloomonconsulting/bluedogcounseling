Consulting for staff wellbeing and retention

Some jobs ask people to absorb the public's hardest moments as a routine part of the work. BLUE dog consults with organizations whose staff carry those demands, on the policies, resources, and practices that support wellbeing and retention.

Who this is for

Professions where loss is routine

Hospice and palliative care, emergency medical services, hospital and ICU staff, child welfare, crisis response, and funeral services. Staff in these roles encounter death, trauma, and family grief regularly.

Community-based and public-facing roles

Libraries, schools, social services, municipal offices, and community-based organizations. Staff in these roles absorb difficult public interactions as a routine part of the job.

What consulting covers

Staff wellbeing and retention

Identifying the workplace conditions that drive turnover and developing practical supports that help experienced staff stay. Retention is treated as a structural question, not an individual one.

Emotional demands of the work

Naming what staff absorb in public-facing and caregiving roles, and building responses into policy and team practice.

Moral stress and secondary trauma

For organizations where staff encounter death, trauma, or decisions made under constraint, consulting addresses moral stress and secondary traumatic stress directly.

How we work

Every engagement is tailored to the setting, staffing, and specific demands of your organization. We work with leadership and with staff, and the recommendations that come out of it are practical ones your organization can actually implement.

Training is available as a standalone session or as part of a broader consulting project, in person or virtually.

Cōlleen O'Brien, LMSW, is a veterinary social worker whose clinical expertise in grief, moral stress, and secondary traumatic stress applies across settings where people carry emotional weight as part of their job.
