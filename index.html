For animal care professionals

Built specifically for the people doing this work

The people who care for animals regularly support others through grief, loss, uncertainty, and difficult decisions while managing the emotional impact of the work themselves. BLUE Dog provides consulting and training grounded in clinical expertise in grief, animal loss, and the human-animal bond. Our services help animal care organizations create practical, sustainable ways to support the people doing this important work.

See what we offer providers
  • Moral Stress and Secondary Trauma We address the emotional impact of working with animal illness, euthanasia, death, and client grief, helping teams recognize and respond to these challenges in healthy, sustainable ways.
  • Staff Retention and Team Sustainability Supporting staff wellbeing is an important part of retaining skilled, compassionate professionals. We help organizations identify workplace stressors and develop practical supports that strengthen teams.
  • Grief-Informed Client Communication Training that provides a structured, compassionate approach to supporting clients through diagnosis conversations, end-of-life decision-making, euthanasia, and grief.
  • Clinical Expertise in Animal Loss and Grief All consulting and training are grounded in clinical best practices related to grief, animal loss, the human-animal bond, and the unique realities of animal care work.

The practice

Built at the intersection of grief and animal care

Since 2009, Cōlleen O'Brien, LMSW, has provided specialized support for people navigating pet loss, anticipatory grief, and the challenges that can accompany caring for an animal. Through that work, she repeatedly encountered animal care professionals carrying the emotional weight of grief, loss, euthanasia, and moral stress with few resources designed specifically for their experiences.

BLUE Dog's consulting and training services grew from that recognition and from a commitment to supporting the people who care for animals. Since 2010, Cōlleen has facilitated the pet loss support group at the Humane Society of Huron Valley, a partnership that continues to inform and shape the practice today.
