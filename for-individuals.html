What these sessions address

Pet loss and grief

Grief after the death of an animal family member is real loss. These sessions provide structured, clinically informed support for processing that grief, including grief that others in your life may minimize or not understand.

Euthanasia decisions

Deciding when and whether to euthanize a beloved animal is one of the most difficult decisions a person can face. The weight of that decision, and the grief and guilt that often follow, deserve clinical attention. Consultation provides space to process both the decision and what comes after it.

Terminal diagnosis and caregiving

When an animal family member receives a serious diagnosis, the anticipatory grief and caregiving stress that follows is significant. These sessions can help you understand your options, manage that stress, and identify a support plan while your animal is still with you.

Complex or unacknowledged grief

Grief after animal loss is frequently minimized by the people around you. That minimization compounds the loss. Consultation provides a space where the significance of the relationship and the grief are taken seriously from the start.

Individual support services

Support for people navigating pet loss, anticipatory grief, difficult medical or end-of-life decisions, and caregiving stress. BLUE Dog offers a free 15-minute consultation to explore whether services are a good fit.

Telehealth sessions are available in 30-, 45-, and 60-minute formats with a clinician who specializes in grief and the human-animal bond. Sessions include time to review your situation in clinical terms, ask questions, and identify the most useful resources and supports.

The grief associated with pet loss and end-of-life decisions is frequently minimized in standard clinical settings. These sessions provide a structured space where the clinical weight of that loss is taken seriously.
